How to Play EyeTic 👁️

1

Position Yourself

Sit approximately 40 – 70 cm directly facing your webcam in comfortable lighting.

2

Look to Target

Shift your gaze toward any of the 9 cells on the board. The system detects your pupil landmarks in real-time.

3

Hold to Select (Dwell)

Hold your gaze steady on the cell. The circular green progress ring will fill up. Once full, your mark (X) is placed!

4

Accessibility & Fallback Controls

You can also use Mouse clicks or keyboard Arrow keys + Enter / Space to play at any time.

Settings ⚙️

Sound Effects

Camera Access & Permissions 📷

1

Allow In Browser (Chrome / Edge / Firefox)

Click the Authorize Camera In Browser button, or click the 🔒 Padlock / Camera icon on the left side of your browser URL bar (next to localhost:5000) and set Camera: Allow.

2

Windows Camera Privacy Setting

If your camera still doesn't turn on, open Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Camera:

  • Ensure Camera access is toggled ON
  • Ensure Let apps access your camera is toggled ON
  • Ensure Let desktop apps access your camera is toggled ON (for Python)
3

Close Other Apps Using the Camera

On Windows, only one application can access the webcam at a time. Close Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Discord, or any other camera app.
